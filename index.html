SPARKBORN Privacy Policy

App: SPARKBORN (Android package com.azozcorp.sparkborn)
Developer: Azooz corp
Contact: maxyc33@gmail.com
Effective date: 4 November 2026

1. Summary

SPARKBORN is an offline incremental game. It has no accounts and no online features of its own. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The game can show ads (Google AdMob) and, only if you allow it, send anonymous usage statistics. You can remove forced ads with a one-time purchase when that purchase is offered.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings and your privacy choices are saved in a local file on your device. We do not receive this file. Uninstalling the game, or using "Reset progress" in Settings, deletes it.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: your IP address, product interactions (such as app opens and taps), diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can review your choice later in the game's Settings (Ad privacy choices) where it is offered.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Reward videos are always optional. The game is fully playable without watching any ad.
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Usage statistics (optional)

When the game first starts it asks whether you allow anonymous usage statistics. If you accept, the game sends anonymous events (for example "stage reached" or session length) to Google Firebase Analytics to help us improve the game. If you decline, nothing is sent, and the game plays exactly the same. You can change your choice at any time in Settings.

5. Purchases

Purchases, when offered, are handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened, which we verify to unlock what you bought; we never receive your payment details.

6. Children

SPARKBORN is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, delete or object to the use of your data, and to withdraw consent. We do not hold your game data. Requests about advertising or analytics data are handled by Google; you can also contact us at maxyc33@gmail.com and we will help where we can.

8. Security and transfers

Data sent by the advertising and analytics services is encrypted in transit. These providers may process data in other countries under their own safeguards. The game stores no passwords or payment details.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do, and the current version will always be available at this address.
